Welcome to LDECAIO!

Welcome to the Laboratory for Data Engineering, Computational Analytics and Intelligent Optimization. We explore data-driven methods, computational techniques, and intelligent approaches to solving challenging problems.

About the Laboratory

Our laboratory focuses on developing and applying methods for collecting, managing, processing, and analysing data, alongside computational models and optimization techniques for complex real-world problems.

Through research, experimentation, and collaboration, we aim to contribute to reliable data systems, effective analytical methods, and intelligent decision-making.
